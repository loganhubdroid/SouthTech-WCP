What is System Engineering?

Systems engineering is designing, integrating, and managing complex systems throughout their lifecycle. System engineers coordinate multiple components to ensure all parts work together effectively, from initial requirements through deployment and maintenance. The field spans many industries, requiring both technical skills and project management skills.

The kind of salary you could expect from System Engineers can vary drastically. Entry level can earn around $70-$85 thousand dollars a year, mid level could earn $93-$125 thousand dollars a year, and senior levels can earn $130-$155 thousands dollars a year.

Before someone starts their field as a System Engineer, there are, of course, a couple skills that they would need to know. Those being:

  • Basic knowloedge of Operating Systems
  • Programming and Scripting
  • Networking and Cloud
  • System Integration & lifecycle
  • Risk and configuration management

Welcome to our schools Art Club!

We mainly start the club with a short 10 minute or less meeting, followed by, depending on the day, drawing or painting in some form of way

Since our club goes on through the entirety of the Second Semester, we have quite a bit of time to work with. Because of this, we go through different ways of art, think of it like units in class!

    Some, it not all, of the ones we do are:
  • Classic painting on a canvas
  • Painting with colored pencils and markers
  • Water Coloring
  • Digital art using an optional drawing tablet

Unlike actual boring school though, we dont do these one at a time and do them for months at a time. Towards the end of meetings we vote on which we will do for the day, and sometimes, depending on how many times we've done it, we will add chalenges or promps to the day.

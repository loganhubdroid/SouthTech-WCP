Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system. It's only slightly larger than Earth's moon. Tempterature on Mercury can reach up to 800oF during the day, and as low as -290oF At night.

Name: The Sun is from an acient german word that means glowing star in the sky.

Life: The Sun is too hot for life to exist on the sun, as it gets p 10,000oF Just on the surface, and the core reaches 27 millionoF.

Size: The Sun has a diameter of about 865,000 miles which is 1.4 million Kilometers. Making it roughly 109 times wider than Earths.

Rotation: The Sun rotates on its axis as it spins through space, although it being a giant ball of gas and not a solid object, different parts of the sun rotate at different rates.

Moons: The Sun doesn't have any Moons or Rings.

Formation: The Sun formed roughly 4.6 Billion years ago in a giant spinning cloud of gas called the solar nebula. As the nebula collapsed untder its gravity it spun until its materials were pulled toward the center and formed the Sun.

Structure: The Sun is a huge ball of hydrogen, held together by its own gravity. It is about 86,ooo miles thick and the core is about 8 times denser than the density of gold.

Surface: The Sun doesnt have a solid surface like Earth. The part of the sun commonly referred to as its surface is the photosphere because this layer emits the most visible light.

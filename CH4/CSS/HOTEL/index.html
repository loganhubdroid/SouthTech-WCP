The Frost Bite Hotel

Welcome to The Frost Bite Hotel, located in Atka, Alaska, a small little quaint bay area surrounded by water on one side and mountains on another.

Inside our hotel, we have plenty of pleasing things to keep you entertained at your temporary stay here. Such as our...

  • Our cozy warm hot tub or pools.
  • Or check out our food court if you arise from your slumber which has a blasting AC.
  • You could take a relaxing ride down our cave boat ride which we can afford.

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system. It's only slightly larger than Earth's moon. Tempterature on Mercury can reach up to 800oF during the day, and as low as -290oF At night.

Name: Mercury got it's not from the Swiftest Roman God because of it orbiting the sun in 88 days.

Life: Mercury's environment is not conducive to life as the temperatures and and solar radiation on the planet are too extreme for organisms to adapt to.

Size and Distance: With a radius of 15 hundred miles, Mercury is a little more than a third of Earths width. Mercury is also 0.4 Astromical units away from the Sun, one AU is the distance from the Sun to the Earth.

Rotation: Mercury spins slowly on its axis and completes one rotation every 59 Earth days. Mercury's axis of rotation is tilted just 2 degrees with respect to the plane of its orbit around the Sun. That means it spins nearly perfectly upright and so does not experience seasons as many other planets do.

Moons: Mercury doesn't have any Moons or Rings.

Formation: Mercury formed roughly 4.5 Billion years ago when gravity pulling swirling gas and dust together to form this small planet closest to the Sun. Like other planets, it has a core, a mantle, and a solid crust.

Structure: Mercury is the second densest planet, right after Earth. There is evidence that it is partly molten or liquid. Mercury's outer shell is only about 400 Kilometers thick.

Surface: The surface is very similar to Earth's moon with it being scarred by many craters. While most of it is smooth terrain, there also cliffs, some hundreds of miles long and soaring up to a mile high. Mercury may have ice water at its north and south poles deep inside craters, but in regions premanent shadows. It could be cold enough to preserve ice water despite the high temperatures.
